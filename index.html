How to play

Claim hexes by matching a clue to its letter. You (green) connect top to bottom; a computer opponent (red) connects left to right whenever you answer one wrong.

Start from any hex on the top or bottom row — after that first pick, you can only choose a hex adjacent to one you already own, so build outward one connected step at a time. A couple of hexes on every board are permanently dropped and can't be claimed by either side, so route around them.

Works with any subject: use the bundled sample deck or bring your own via Upload.
